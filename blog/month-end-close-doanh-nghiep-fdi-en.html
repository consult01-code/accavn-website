Month-End Close for FDI Companies: A 5-Working-Day Group Calendar

A 5-working-day month-end close calendar for FDI companies, with owners, controls and tips to shorten the close.

Most groups require subsidiaries to submit monthly figures within the first few working days of the following month. To complete the month-end close on time and accurately, an FDI company needs a fixed calendar by working day (WD), with clear owners and control points. Below is the 5-day calendar the accounting team at ACCA Consulting Vietnam typically applies; you can adapt it to your group's deadlines.

1. Why you need a fixed month-end close calendar

Without a calendar, the close tends to drag on while waiting for invoices, bank statements or payroll approval. The result is late group reporting or adjustments the following month. A fixed calendar ensures that:

  • Departments (purchasing, sales, HR) know their deadlines for sending documents to accounting.
  • Accounting has time to reconcile before closing, reducing adjustments the following month.
  • Monthly figures are ready before the monthly value-added tax (VAT/GTGT) return deadline (the 20th of the following month) or the quarterly one, avoiding returns prepared on unclosed figures.

4. Tips to shorten the close with automation

No big project is needed. These small steps usually save significant time:

  • Connect bank data to the accounting software (if the bank and software support it) instead of keying in statements manually.
  • Download e-invoices in bulk and match them automatically against the supplier list, instead of opening each email.
  • Template accrual schedules for recurring expenses (office rent, electricity, internet, service fees).
  • A fixed account mapping table so the reporting package is generated from the trial balance; see mapping the VAS chart of accounts to the group.
  • A data dashboard for fluctuation analysis; see also automating group reporting.

The example shows that most of the savings come from repetitive tasks (bank, invoices). Analyzing and explaining fluctuations still requires experienced people. When using AI tools, do not upload payroll or contract data to public tools.

FAQ

Does a small company need to close in 5 days?

It is not mandatory. But FDI companies with group reporting should have a fixed calendar; companies with few transactions can use a 3-day calendar.

Does FX revaluation have to be done every month?

The accounting regime requires it at the financial reporting date. Many groups require it monthly so that management figures stay close to reality.

Can an outsourced provider meet a WD5 deadline?

Yes, if the contract clearly states the calendar, the company's document deadlines and SLA metrics. See the article on SLAs in accounting service contracts.

How should invoices arriving after the period is locked be handled?

If an accrual was made, record the invoice and reverse the accrual in the next period. If not, assess materiality to decide which period to record it in.
