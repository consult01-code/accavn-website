Investment Project Implementation Reports in Vietnam: How to File

A guide to the investment project implementation report: reporting periods, figures to take from the accounting records and common capital reporting errors.

For FDI companies, the investment project implementation report (báo cáo tình hình thực hiện dự án đầu tư) is a periodic obligation to the investment registration authority, entirely separate from tax returns. Many companies file on time but report the wrong capital figures, so the data does not match the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC – Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký đầu tư) or the financial statements. This article explains how to take the figures from the books, based on the experience of the accounting services team at ACCA Consulting Vietnam.

1. What is the investment project implementation report?

It is a report that a foreign-invested economic organization submits to the investment registration authority on the progress of its project: capital contributed, capital disbursed, revenue, employees, payments to the state budget and other indicators. The report is filed on the National Investment Information System using an account issued by the investment registration authority.

There are quarterly and annual reports; the specific deadline for each period is set out in the regulations implementing the Law on Investment and may change when the law is amended. Companies should check directly on the system or ask the investment registration authority to confirm the schedule, rather than relying on outdated information.

2. Indicators to take from the accounting records

Most indicators are already available in the accounting records. The accountant's job is to identify the correct source and use a consistent method across periods, so that next year's figures do not contradict the previous year's.

3. How to file the report on the system

The system interface may change with each upgrade. If the company has several projects, a separate report is prepared for each project; take care to select the correct project code before entering figures.

4. Common errors when reporting capital figures

  • Confusing cumulative figures with amounts for the period: the most common error, which inflates disbursed capital period after period.
  • Reporting committed capital instead of contributed capital: the reported figure exceeds the balance of the direct investment capital account.
  • Inconsistent units and exchange rates: USD in one period, VND at a different exchange rate in the next.
  • Not consistent with the capital contribution timeline: capital must be contributed within 90 days of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) issue date; a report showing late contribution without an adjustment is a red flag. See capital contribution deadlines.
  • Revenue that differs from the tax return: caused by taking figures before period-end adjusting entries.

5. Reminders and responsibilities

Add the investment report to the same calendar as your tax deadlines so it is not forgotten. The annual report should be prepared after the financial statement figures are finalized, as many indicators are taken directly from the financial statements. See also the overview of FDI company reporting obligations for all the year's obligations.

FAQ

Does an FDI company with no revenue yet have to file the investment project implementation report?

Yes. The reporting obligation is tied to the licensed investment project, regardless of whether there is revenue. Periods with no activity still require reporting of contributed capital, disbursed capital and employees.

Who holds the account for filing on the system?

The account is issued by the investment registration authority to the economic organization. The company should assign a fixed person in charge and hand it over when staff or the accounting service provider changes.

What if incorrect figures have already been submitted?

Contact the investment registration authority for guidance on corrections. The company should keep an explanation of the differences and the corrected worksheet for reconciliation.
