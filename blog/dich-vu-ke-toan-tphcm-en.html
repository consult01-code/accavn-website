There are many providers of accounting services in Ho Chi Minh City, from freelancers working on the side to firms with their own office and team. Prices vary widely, and ways of working even more so. This article sets out 8 concrete criteria for choosing a reliable accounting firm, together with a quick scorecard and the published fee schedule of ACCA Consulting Vietnam as a benchmark.

1. Why choosing the right accounting provider in Ho Chi Minh City matters

When you outsource, you hand an external party work that directly affects your tax payments and legal records. Yet responsibility towards the tax authority remains with your company. Late returns, invalid invoices or books lacking supporting documents can all lead to penalties, late-payment interest or tax arrears during a finalization audit.

Accounting records used for bookkeeping and preparing financial statements must also be kept for at least 10 years. Choose the wrong provider and switching later usually means paying for a review and rebuilding the books. A careful evaluation at the outset is always cheaper than fixing mistakes afterwards.

2. 8 criteria for choosing a reliable accounting firm

Criterion 1: A clearly identifiable legal entity

Prefer a provider that is a registered company with a verifiable tax code, a physical office address, and that signs the service contract as a legal entity. This gives you a basis for holding it accountable if needed.

Criterion 2: Transparent pricing

A good quotation states clearly that fees are based on invoice and document volume, industry and headcount. All surcharges should be listed up front — not announced at year-end as an extra fee for financial statements or tax finalization.

Criterion 3: A contract with a clearly defined scope

The contract should list what is and is not included: periodic tax returns, bookkeeping, financial statements, tax finalization, payroll and social insurance. Reading this section carefully avoids misunderstandings later.

Criterion 4: A document handover process

A professional firm has a document collection schedule, a fixed channel for sending files, and sends reminders before every filing period. You should not have to remember the deadlines yourself.

Criterion 5: Regular reporting to the business owner

Before each filing, you should receive the estimated tax payable for approval. Periodically, the provider should also summarize revenue, expenses and any points requiring attention.

Criterion 6: Industry and entity-type experience

Accounting for trading differs from manufacturing or construction; FDI companies also need independently audited financial statements and communication in English. Ask whether the provider has worked with businesses like yours.

Criterion 7: Confidentiality and handover

The contract should include a data confidentiality clause and a commitment to hand over all books and documents when the engagement ends. Many companies overlook this point.

Criterion 8: Support during tax authority reviews

When you receive a request for explanation or a finalization audit notice, the provider should help prepare figures and supporting files within the agreed scope. Ask whether this support is charged separately.
