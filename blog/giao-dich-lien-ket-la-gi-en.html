What Are Related Party Transactions in Vietnam? Spotting Them

Related party transactions explained in plain language: how to identify related parties and which transactions are commonly overlooked.

Almost every FDI company has related party transactions: buying goods from the parent company, paying management fees, borrowing from the group. The problem is that many of these transactions are not labelled as "purchases" or "sales", so they are easily missed in tax filings, even though this is a group of transactions the tax authority pays particular attention to. This article explains the concept, how to identify related parties and how to build a tracking list. It was prepared by the accounting team at ACCA Consulting Vietnam.

1. What is a related party transaction?

A related party transaction (giao dịch liên kết) is a transaction between parties that have a related party relationship. Two parties are considered related when one participates directly or indirectly in the management, control or capital of the other, or when both are under the management or control of a third party. Because prices between such parties may not be set by the market, tax law requires these transactions to be declared and priced according to the arm's length principle.

2. Identifying related parties: 4 types of relationship

The current regulations list many specific cases, each tied to a certain level of ownership, guarantee value or loan amount. Here we group them into four easy-to-remember categories; when filing, the accountant must check each criterion and threshold in detail against the regulations in force.

  • Ownership relationships: parent company, subsidiaries, "sister" companies under the same parent, and investors holding capital directly or indirectly.
  • Management relationships: companies sharing a director or members of the management board, or controlled by the same group of people.
  • Financial relationships: a party that guarantees or lends an amount that is large relative to equity or total debt, according to the prescribed thresholds.
  • Family and contractual relationships: companies run by relatives of the company's managers; business arrangements that allow one party to control the other.

FAQ

Do domestic companies have related party transactions?

Yes. The concept is not limited to FDI companies; a Vietnamese company with subsidiaries or companies under common ownership can also have them.

Do transactions with no payment need to be declared?

They need to be reviewed. Transactions such as free-of-charge guarantees or seconded staff can still be related party transactions and affect tax obligations.

What ownership percentage makes a party related?

The specific thresholds are set out in the current regulations on tax administration for enterprises with related party transactions. Check the latest regulations or contact an adviser.

Where can we get information on related parties?

From the parent company (group chart), enterprise registration documents, the list of managers, and loan and guarantee agreements.
