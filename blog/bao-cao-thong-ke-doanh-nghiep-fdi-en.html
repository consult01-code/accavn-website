Accountants at FDI companies usually have a firm grip on the tax calendar but can easily miss statistical reports for FDI companies and other reports submitted to authorities other than the tax office. These reports rarely trigger immediate penalties, but during an inspection or when amending an investment project, missing reports will slow the file down. This article groups the main non-tax reports and explains how ACCA Consulting Vietnam sets up reminders for its clients.

1. Why non-tax reports are so often forgotten

Tax returns come with reminder systems, clearly defined penalties, and the accountant prepares them every month. Statistical reports, labor reports and foreign loan reports, by contrast, are usually semi-annual or annual, go to different authorities, and nobody in the company is clearly assigned to them. When the accountant or the service provider changes, these obligations are very easy to lose.

5. Assign responsibilities clearly

  • Accounting: financial data, capital, revenue, loans.
  • HR: labor data, expats, work permits.
  • Legal or the director: track regulatory changes, sign and submit reports.
  • Accounting service provider: if you use one, state clearly in the contract which reports are in scope. See also the investment project implementation report.

A practical tip: keep a one-page reporting obligations register listing each report's name, authority, period, person in charge and where the filed copy is stored. At a staff handover, passing on this single page is enough for the newcomer to see the whole picture.

FAQ

Does a small FDI company have to submit statistical reports?

It depends on the statistical reporting regime in force and whether the company is on a survey list. The safe approach is to check with the local statistics office every year.

Is the foreign employee report different from a work permit application?

Yes. A work permit application is a procedure for each individual, while the report on the use of foreign workers is a periodic obligation of the company under the regulations.

Do accounting service providers handle non-tax reports?

It depends on the scope of the contract. At ACCA Consulting Vietnam, non-tax reports are listed clearly in the accounting service quotation so the company knows exactly what is covered.
