Every corporate income tax (CIT/TNDN) finalization season, FDI accountants spend days completing the related party transaction appendix. The difficulty is not the form but the data: amounts are scattered across many accounts and currencies, and they must match the related party note in the audited financial statements. This article shows how to organize the data so the appendix is quick and consistent, following the approach used by the accounting team at ACCA Consulting Vietnam.

5. How does the appendix differ from transfer pricing documentation?

The appendix is a data disclosure filed with the finalization. Transfer pricing documentation is a set of analytical documents proving that prices comply with the arm's length principle; it is kept at the company and provided on request. A company may be exempt from preparing documentation but still be required to file the appendix. Details are in transfer pricing documentation for related party transactions.

FAQ

If there were no related party transactions during the year, must the appendix still be filed?

It depends on the current rules on who must declare. If the company has related party relationships but no transactions, check the latest guidance or ask the tax authority.

Which exchange rate should be used for foreign currency transactions?

Use the exchange rates recorded in the accounting books consistently, so the appendix figures match the books and the financial statements.

Must the appendix match the financial statement notes exactly?

It should. If there are differences due to classification, prepare a reconciliation explaining them and keep it with the file.
