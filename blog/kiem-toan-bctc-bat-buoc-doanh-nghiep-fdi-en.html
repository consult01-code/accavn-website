Mandatory FDI Financial Statement Audit: A 12-Week Plan

A 12-week schedule that helps FDI accounting teams complete the annual financial statement audit before 31 March without a last-week crunch.

The audit of an FDI company's financial statements is a mandatory annual obligation carried out by an independent audit firm. For a financial year ending 31 December, the annual financial statements must be filed by 31 March of the following year at the latest, which in practice leaves only about 12 weeks for both closing the books and the audit. This article breaks those 12 weeks into concrete tasks, following the approach the accounting team at ACCA Consulting Vietnam uses with its FDI clients.

1. Why are FDI financial statement audits so often late?

Audits are rarely late because the audit firm is slow. The cause usually lies with the company: figures not yet closed, documents sent piecemeal, and drawn-out debates over audit adjustments. Meanwhile, many other tasks fall due at the same time: the corporate income tax (CIT/TNDN) finalization, the personal income tax (PIT/TNCN) finalization and reporting to the parent company.

The four quarterly provisional CIT payments together must not be less than 80% of the tax payable per the finalization. If the audit significantly increases profit and this is discovered late, the company may have to pay late-payment interest on the shortfall. That alone is reason enough to start early.

3. The PBC list: documents to send to the auditors

PBC (Prepared by Client) is the list of documents the company prepares itself. Sending everything in one go, with consistent file names, shortens fieldwork considerably.

4. Handling audit adjustments

Audit adjustments are the part most likely to cause disputes. An effective approach:

  1. Ask the auditors to send their list of proposed adjustments early rather than at the end of fieldwork.
  2. Classify them: adjustments agreed immediately, adjustments requiring more documents, and adjustments not agreed.
  3. For adjustments you do not agree with, prepare your reasoning and supporting documents in writing.
  4. Update the books and the CIT finalization return immediately so the two sets of figures match.
  5. Keep the signed agreement minutes to explain the position to the parent company and the tax authority.
File the CIT finalization only after the audit adjustments have been agreed. Filing first and then submitting a supplementary return is time-consuming and may trigger late-payment interest of 0.03% per day.

FAQ

Can a small FDI company with low revenue be exempt from audit?

No. Foreign-invested enterprises are subject to a mandatory annual financial statement audit regardless of revenue size.

If the financial year differs from the calendar year, how is the deadline calculated?

The deadline is the last day of the third month after the end of the financial year. The 12-week schedule shifts accordingly.

Should the audit engagement be signed after the books are closed?

It is better to sign before the financial year ends, so the auditors can attend the stock counts and you can secure their schedule during peak season.
