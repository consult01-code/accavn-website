Mandatory Accounting and Tax Reports for FDI Companies

Which accounting and tax reports an FDI company must file during the year, and how to organize the calendar so nothing is late.

FDI company reporting goes beyond that of domestic companies in two ways: annual financial statements must be independently audited, and there are additional reports on the investment project. This article lists the reports you definitely have to prepare, the statutory deadlines that are clearly established, and how to organize the work. It is compiled by the accounting team at ACCA Consulting Vietnam from experience working with foreign-invested companies.

1. Overview of FDI company reporting

An FDI company is a Vietnamese legal entity, so first and foremost it bears the same accounting and tax obligations as a domestic company. For audit reports and investment project reports, the specific deadlines should be checked against the latest regulations and your company's project. Note that the 2025 Law on Tax Administration takes effect from 1 July 2026, so new guidance needs to be followed.

2. Periodic tax returns: monthly or quarterly?

Newly established companies file value-added tax (VAT/GTGT) returns quarterly. From the following year, companies whose total revenue from goods and services in the preceding year was VND 50 billion or less may file quarterly; above VND 50 billion they file monthly. Personal income tax (PIT/TNCN) withholding returns follow the same period as VAT; no PIT return is required for a month or quarter with no tax withheld.

For corporate income tax (CIT/TNDN), companies do not file quarterly returns but must make quarterly provisional payments. Total provisional payments for the four quarters must not be less than 80% of the CIT payable per the annual finalization. FDI companies often have expenses involving the parent company, so profit should be estimated carefully to make sufficient provisional payments.

3. Audited annual financial statements

This is the biggest difference. FDI companies must have their annual financial statements audited by an independent audit firm, even if they are small or have no revenue yet. The accountant prepares the financial statements; the audit firm issues an opinion. The two roles are separate: the firm that keeps the books cannot audit those same books.

Auditors usually focus on: capital contributed through the DICA (direct investment capital account), balances with the parent company, year-end revenue and expenses, fixed assets, payroll and social insurance. If the books are not reconciled regularly during the year, a large number of adjusting entries pile up at year-end and there is a risk of a qualified opinion.

5. Common risks in FDI company reporting

  • Late returns: under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP and its amendments and supplements, an organization filing a tax return 1–30 days late may be fined VND 2–5 million; penalties increase with the number of days late.
  • Insufficient provisional CIT: the four quarters total less than 80% of the finalized amount.
  • Late tax payment: late-payment interest of 0.03% per day on the overdue tax.
  • Loss of VAT deduction: invoices of VND 5 million or more paid in cash.
  • Books that don't match the audited financial statements: figures declared for tax differ from post-audit figures, requiring supplementary returns.
  • Incomplete record-keeping: documents used for bookkeeping and financial statements must be kept for at least 10 years under the 2015 Law on Accounting.

FAQ

Does an FDI company with no revenue still have to file reports?

Yes. The company must still prepare annual financial statements, have them audited as required and file reports related to its investment project. VAT returns are still filed each period under current regulations; PIT returns, however, are not required for periods with no tax withheld.

When are an FDI company's financial statements and CIT finalization due?

No later than the last day of the 3rd month after the financial year-end. For a calendar financial year, that is 31 March.

Does ACCA audit financial statements for FDI companies?

No. ACCA provides accounting and prepares reports and returns, then coordinates with the independent audit firm chosen by the company.

When is the investment project implementation report due?

This report is filed periodically under current investment regulations. Deadlines and forms should be checked against the latest documents; ACCA can help review the requirements for each project.
