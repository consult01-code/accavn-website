Profit Repatriation for FDI Companies in Vietnam: Conditions

The sequence for remitting profits abroad from an FDI company: required conditions, the order of documents and the points banks most often query.

For a foreign investor, repatriating profits is the moment when business results in Vietnam become real cash for the parent company. But the transfer order is only the final step: before that, the company must have audited financial statements, a completed tax finalization and a valid profit distribution decision. This article sets out the whole chain of tasks in the correct order, based on the accounting experience of ACCA Consulting Vietnam with foreign-invested clients.

1. Conditions for remitting profits abroad

Investment law allows foreign investors to remit profits abroad once they have fulfilled their financial obligations to the Vietnamese State. In practice, banks and the tax authority look at the three groups of conditions below. Specific details (notification timing, forms) apply under current regulations, so the company should re-check them before each remittance.

A frequently overlooked point is the accumulated loss condition. If the company made losses in its early years, this year's profit must first offset those past losses. This situation is analysed with figures in can profits be remitted with accumulated losses?

2. Six steps from closing the books to the transfer order

The order matters because each step relies on the result of the previous one. For example, the distribution decision must be based on audited figures, not provisional ones. If post-audit adjustments reduce profit, a decision made earlier may exceed the permitted amount.

3. Typical timeline during the year

For a financial year matching the calendar year, the annual financial statements and CIT finalization are due no later than 31 March of the following year. As a result, the previous year's profit can usually only be remitted from Q2 onwards. Companies that need funds sent to the parent company sooner should bring the audit schedule forward.

4. Example: calculating the amount that can be remitted

Assume a single-member foreign-owned limited liability company with no accumulated losses, accounting profit before tax for 2025 of VND 2 billion (≈ USD 78,400, approximate, at VND 25,500/USD), no disallowed expenses, and the standard CIT rate of 20%. The owner decides to retain VND 400 million as working capital.

Note: profit distributed to the investor may give rise to other tax obligations depending on whether the investor is an organization or an individual. Determine this before remitting to avoid having to make supplementary filings later.

5. Checklist of documents banks typically require

Each bank has its own list, but most require the documents below. Preparing English translations in parallel helps the parent company approve faster.

Banks often reject a transfer when the beneficiary name does not match the investor's name on the IRC, or when it is sent from an ordinary current account instead of the direct investment capital account (DICA).

6. Common mistakes and how to prevent them

  • Distributing based on unaudited figures: wait for the final audit report before issuing the distribution decision.
  • Forgetting small tax debts: a few hundred thousand dong of late-payment interest is still a tax debt; check your obligations before submitting documents.
  • Remitting profits mid-year: discuss carefully with your bank and advisers, as in most cases profits are only remitted after the annual finalization.
  • Incomplete distribution decisions: state the profit year, amount, currency, expected exchange rate and beneficiary clearly.

FAQ

Can an FDI company remit profits every quarter?

Profits are usually remitted after the financial year-end, once audited financial statements and the tax finalization are in place. If you wish to remit during the year, check current regulations and consult your bank in advance.

Must the remittance go through the DICA?

Yes. Capital and profit transfers by foreign investors are made through the direct investment capital account opened at a licensed bank.

Can profit not remitted this year be remitted next year?

Yes. Undistributed profit after tax can be distributed in a later period, provided that at the time of distribution the tax obligation conditions are still met and no accumulated losses arise.
