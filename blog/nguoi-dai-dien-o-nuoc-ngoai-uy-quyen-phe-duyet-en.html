Director Based Abroad: Authorization, Digital Signatures, Approvals

Approval matrices, authorization and digital signature management when the legal representative is often outside Vietnam.

Many FDI companies have a general director who also covers the region and spends only a few days a month in Vietnam. When the legal representative is based abroad, the practical questions are: who signs tax filings, who holds the digital signature, who approves payment orders, and how do you avoid one person holding all the power? This article explains how to design an authorization matrix and internal controls, based on the FDI accounting experience of ACCA Consulting Vietnam.

1. When the legal representative is abroad: key requirements

The Law on Enterprises requires a company to always have a legal representative residing in Vietnam; when the sole legal representative leaves the country, they must authorize another person in writing to exercise their rights and obligations during their absence. The specific conditions, scope and consequences should be checked against current regulations and the company charter.

Operationally, authorization is only the legal layer. The more important layer is who does what, up to what limit, with which tools: the digital signature for tax filing, bank tokens, the company seal and approval rights in the accounting software.

3. Managing digital signatures and bank tokens

  • The company digital signature used for tax returns and e-invoices: held by the accountant, with a handover log and password changes when staff change.
  • Bank tokens/OTP: set up two levels, “maker” and “approver”; the approver can be the general director approving remotely.
  • Never share one token or one e-banking account for both creating and approving payment orders.
  • Transaction limits in e-banking should match the internal approval matrix.
  • Revoke immediately digital signatures, tokens and access rights when staff leave or an authorization expires.

FAQ

Does a legal representative living abroad need to authorize someone?

Under the Law on Enterprises, a company must have a legal representative residing in Vietnam; when the sole legal representative leaves the country, a written authorization is required. Check the charter and current regulations for your specific case.

Who should hold the digital signature for tax filing?

It is usually held by the accountant or the accounting service provider under a handover record, since it is needed every month. Approval rights for transfers in e-banking should be kept separately with an authorized person.

Can the director approve payments remotely?

Yes, if the e-banking system and software allow separate maker and approver roles. What matters is that the system limits match the internal approval matrix.
