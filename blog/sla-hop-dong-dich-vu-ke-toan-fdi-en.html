SLAs in Accounting Service Contracts for FDI Companies

The SLA clauses to include when outsourcing accounting for an FDI company, with measurable sample KPIs and how to track them.

Accounting service contracts often say little more than "Party B performs accounting and tax filing for Party A". For an FDI company with group reporting deadlines and an annual audit, that is far too vague to manage. An accounting service SLA (Service Level Agreement) turns expectations into measurable indicators. This article lists the clauses you should include, compiled by the accounting services team at ACCA Consulting Vietnam.

1. What is an accounting service SLA and where does it sit in the contract?

The SLA is an appendix to the service contract describing what is delivered, when, to what quality and what happens if targets are missed. Keeping the SLA as a separate appendix lets both parties update the indicators each year without amending the main contract.

A good SLA has three features: indicators measurable with data (days, hours, number of errors), reciprocal obligations for the company (deadlines for sending documents), and a periodic review mechanism.

2. Six groups of clauses to include

The company's reciprocal obligations

An SLA is only fair if the company also commits: sending documents by WD2, approving payroll by the agreed date, and granting view-only access to e-banking and e-invoices. If the company sends documents late, the provider's deadlines are pushed back accordingly.

5. Common mistakes when drafting an SLA

  • Unmeasurable indicators: "professional service", "quick response". Replace them with hours, days and error counts.
  • Uncapped penalties: these lead good providers to decline or raise their prices. Include a cap and exclude errors caused by the company.
  • Not stating who signs and files: returns are digitally signed by the company; the provider prepares them and sends them for approval. Spell this out to avoid disputes over responsibility.
  • Forgetting data at contract termination: include a handover clause; see the accounting handover checklist.

FAQ

Does the accounting provider pay tax penalties on behalf of the company?

Legally, the company is the taxpayer and bears penalties vis-à-vis the tax authority. The contract may provide that the provider reimburses penalties caused by its own errors, within an agreed cap.

Should the SLA set response times of a few hours?

It depends on your needs. Most FDI companies choose 1 working day for routine questions, with a separate channel for urgent matters during the finalization season.

Does a small company need an SLA?

Yes, even a short version with 3–4 indicators: return deadlines, reporting deadlines, response time and confidentiality.

How often should the SLA be updated?

Annually, or when transaction volumes, group requirements or tax rules change significantly.
