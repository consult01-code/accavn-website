Amending the IRC in Vietnam: When It's Required and What to File

An FDI company has two “birth certificates”: the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC – Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký đầu tư) for the project and the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC – Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký doanh nghiệp) for the legal entity. When increasing capital, changing address or adding business lines, many accountants update only the ERC and forget to amend the Investment Registration Certificate. The consequences usually surface when the bank refuses a transfer. This article provides a reference table and process, based on the FDI accounting experience of ACCA Consulting Vietnam.

1. What do the IRC and ERC record?

The IRC records information about the investment project: the investor, objectives, scale, investment capital, location, implementation schedule and project term. The ERC records information about the enterprise: name, head office address, business lines, charter capital, members/shareholders and legal representative.

When setting up a new company, a foreign investor usually obtains the IRC first (15 days from receipt of a complete, valid application where investment policy approval is not required), then the ERC (3 working days). The same principle applies to changes: whichever certificate carries the information is the one that must be updated.

4. Consequences of forgetting to amend, especially for transfers

The bank holding the DICA checks every capital transaction against the information on the IRC and ERC. If the company has decided to increase capital but has not amended its certificates, the bank may refuse to accept capital above the registered amount. Conversely, when repatriating profits or withdrawing capital, mismatched information will delay the process.

  • Incorrect invoice details after an address change that has not been updated, making it harder for customers to deduct tax.
  • Project implementation reports that don't match the IRC, likely prompting the authorities to request explanations.
  • Auditors raising issues about contributed capital or project progress in the audit report.

5. License management tips for FDI accountants

Keep a “license register” with the IRC and ERC numbers, issue dates, amendments, registered capital, contribution schedule and project term. Whenever management discusses a capital increase, an office move or new business lines, the accountant should open this register first. For projects with periodic reporting, see also investment project implementation reports.

FAQ

Does changing the legal representative require an IRC amendment?

Usually this is enterprise information, so only the ERC change needs to be registered. However, check whether the project's IRC contains related information before concluding.

How long does an IRC amendment take?

It depends on the type of amendment and whether investment policy approval is required. Allow time for supplementary documents and do not plan transfers before the new certificate is issued.

Can we transfer the additional capital first and amend the IRC later?

This is not advisable. Banks usually check capital transactions against the current certificate, and amounts above the registered capital may be refused or returned.
