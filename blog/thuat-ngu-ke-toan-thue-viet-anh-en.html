Accountants at FDI companies rarely lack technical skill; what they often lack is the precise word when explaining matters to a regional CFO. This article compiles 60 accounting terms in Vietnamese and English used every day, grouped into 4 topics, with sample sentences for emails. The list was selected by the accounting team at ACCA Consulting Vietnam from real work with FDI clients.

1. How to use this accounting glossary

Some Vietnamese concepts have no exact English equivalent, for example "tạm nộp thuế TNDN" or "hoá đơn điện tử có mã" (e-invoice with a tax authority code). For such terms, the safe approach is to use a descriptive English name followed by the Vietnamese abbreviation in brackets on first use, for example provisional CIT payment (tạm nộp TNDN). Once the company has settled on a translation, use it consistently in every report.

2. Tax (Thuế)

Sample sentence:"The provisional CIT payment for Q3 has been made on 28 October, ahead of the deadline on 30 October." (Đã tạm nộp TNDN quý 3 ngày 28/10, trước hạn 30/10.)

3. Financial statements (Báo cáo tài chính)

Sample sentence:"We have booked an accrual of VND 45 million for September utilities, as the invoice will only be issued next month." (Đã trích trước 45 triệu tiền điện nước tháng 9 vì hoá đơn tháng sau mới xuất.)

4. Banking and receivables/payables (Ngân hàng & công nợ)

Sample sentence:"The intercompany loan balance has been revalued at the year-end rate, resulting in an unrealized FX loss of VND 80 million." See the calculation in exchange differences in FDI companies.

5. Payroll and insurance (Lương & bảo hiểm)

Sample sentence:"From the 2026 tax period, the personal deduction has increased to VND 15.5 million per month, so the net pay of most local staff will slightly increase."

FAQ

Should "hoá đơn GTGT" be translated as "VAT invoice" or "red invoice"?

"VAT invoice" or "e-invoice" is more accurate. "Red invoice" is an old term from the days of paper invoices with a red copy; some people still use it, but it can be misleading.

Do you need the international ACCA qualification to use these terms?

No. Note that the international ACCA qualification (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants) is not related to the legal entity ACCA Consulting Vietnam.

How should "quyết toán thuế" be explained to foreigners?

Use "annual tax finalization" and explain that it is the annual return that determines the final tax liability.

How can the whole accounting team keep terminology consistent?

Keep a shared glossary, update it when new terms appear, and use it for report templates and voucher descriptions in the software.
